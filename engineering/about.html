About

An engineer who has actually worked the floor.

I'm Tanatswa Mafume, an industrial engineering student at Wichita State University graduating in December 2026. Most engineers learn the process from the outside. I learned it from the inside, laying up composite parts for aerospace research at NIAR, welding steel frames on contract jobs, and running a water-jet on public installation work.

That's the lens I bring to industrial engineering. When I map a process, I know what the operator is actually doing at each step, where the parts pile up, and why the "official" time standard is wrong. At the GoCreate makerspace I used that to untangle a backlog of roughly 300 pending access requests and an equipment schedule members called unfair. In senior design, I'm on a consulting team improving a production gluing line for Bosch Home Comfort.

I also design and build copper jewelry, furniture and metalwork under my own label, Hove Huru. Running it has taught me costing, sourcing and design for manufacture with my own money on the line, which is the fastest teacher there is.

What I'm looking for

A full-time role starting around December 2026 in manufacturing, operations, supply chain, logistics or construction, somewhere I can improve real processes and be measured on results. I care more about what a role will teach me and the problems I'll own than the title.
